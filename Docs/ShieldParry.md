# Buckler and Spectacular Parry

Shields remain WeaponItemSO assets. Defensive shields have their own AttacksPerSecond for their skill, but contribute no weapon damage or critical chance. IsCombatShield enables offensive weapon stats for future combat shields. Main-hand and offhand skills use the corresponding weapon base speed; global speed modifiers remain effective. With no weapon, base AttackSpeed is 1.

Adventurer's Buckler uses a dedicated 20x20 PixelLab held sprite, 24 PPU, point filtering. Its defensive affix pool copies the 20 affixes from HelmetsEvasionPool into a separate offhand pool. Item identity, inventory icon and implicit modifiers stay intact. The guaranteed primary skill is Spectacular Parry. Existing saved items repair missing granted skills through the existing pool repair path.

Local armor, evasion and mystic-shield modifiers scale only the shield's own defenses before contributing to the character. The shield tooltip displays its calculated attack speed using the existing localized stat row.

Spectacular Parry defaults: 10 mana, 4 s cooldown, base skill speed multiplier 1. The recipe winds up from 0–18%, strikes at 18% (hides the shield sprite), opens ParryWindow and plays VFX from 18–58%, and recovers from 58–80%. The remaining interval finishes the cast. At 1 APS the active window is 0.4 s; speed modifiers scale this with the other recipe steps. All timings and retaliation values are editable in the recipe, mana/cooldown in the skill. Shield Guard and Shield Parry are reusable named hold/swing rows; other weapons' rows are preserved.

During the window every positive direct enemy hit is absorbed, including elemental and projectile hits whose source resolves to EnemyHealth. This also blocks an already launched hit if its attacker has died but its source still exists; resource retaliation skips dead targets. Damage over time, environmental damage and attacks without an identifiable enemy source are excluded. Dodge immunity takes precedence. Successful parries can be tested even with editor immortality enabled. Parried hits return false to damage callers so hit-only ailments are not applied. Window expiry, cancellation, skill removal and disabling the parry component end the guard.

Each successful hit removes exactly 25% of that attacker's maximum stun meter, without damaging HP or using weapon damage or StunBuildUp modifiers. Four full-meter parries break the meter through the normal EnemyStunController stun path. The window continues absorbing hits from stunned enemies until it expires.

GameplayEventType.Parried is appended to preserve serialized enum values. Source is the canonical enemy attacker; Target is the parrying player; Damage is the incoming hit; Amount is raw incoming damage. The player's natural passive filter role is CarrierAsTarget. The skill tree editor exposes Parry in trigger and event-recency selectors.

Passive triggered effects now support EffectTarget (Owner, OtherParticipant, EventSource, EventTarget), inline buff/debuff kind, authored status effects, and DealDamage. CombatTriggerDamage is shared by ParryWindow retaliation and passive reactions: flat HP damage, percentage of maximum HP, flat stun damage, percentage of maximum stun meter. HP effects use the existing pure-damage path; stun effects use ApplyMeterDamage independently. OtherParticipant targets the attacker on Parry. Recursive re-entry of the same passive trigger is blocked. Old effects default to Owner and Buff, preserving existing behavior. Examples: Parry → apply a timed player buff; Parry → debuff OtherParticipant; Parry → deal 5 HP and 10% maximum stun damage to OtherParticipant.
